citation-topics

Research topics and broad areas for any list of works (papers, theses, projects, patents) from their citation links, with Leiden clustering of the direct-citation network as CWTS does for the OpenAlex topics; a board to explore them, and Claude skills that name them.

How the topics are madeThe Leiden algorithm explained step by step, and what the package does exactly. Sample boardThe citation community of INSPIRE-HEP author 991924: 1,967 works in 11 topics. Sample in VOSviewer OnlineThe same network of works, one cluster per topic.

The sample

The papers of the INSPIRE-HEP author 991924 together with every paper citing them, downloaded on 2026-11-15: 79 papers of the author and 1,888 citing papers, with 16,920 citation links among them. Made with:

pip install citation-topics
python inspire_profile.py 991924 works.jsonl     # examples/inspire_profile.py in the repository
citation-topics run works.jsonl out/ --resolution 1 --min-size 20 --seed 4
citation-topics describe works.jsonl out/ --abstracts
citation-topics names out/ names.json            # names written by Claude's topic-namer skill
citation-topics export out/ --works works.jsonl --highlight own --highlight-label "The author's papers"
citation-topics board works.jsonl out/ --highlight own --highlight-label "The author's papers" \
    --title "INSPIRE author 991924: citation topics" \
    --vosviewer-url https://restrepo.github.io/citation-topics/examples/inspire-991924/works_vosviewer.json

The files: names.json, works_vosviewer.json (works) and topics_vosviewer.json (topics), for VOSviewer Online or VOSviewer. A new download from INSPIRE-HEP gives slightly different numbers as new citing papers appear.
